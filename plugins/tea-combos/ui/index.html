Member strategy chooses which member handles a request. Account strategy is separate: it chooses which credential a member is pinned to. inherit leaves account choice to CPA core (no pin); the other two pin accounts and need an accountPool on every member that uses them. A member can override the combo account strategy. quotaPool is an observation pool for scoring only; it never grants or limits account eligibility.
